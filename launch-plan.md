## 4. Readiness checklist

### Product and engineering
- [ ] Must-have requirements delivered and tested
- [ ] Feature flags and rollout controls configured
- [ ] Security, privacy and compliance review completed
- [ ] Known issues documented with workarounds
- [ ] Monitoring and alerting in place

### Partners and integrations
- [ ] Partner integrations tested end to end
- [ ] Partner agreements and joint messaging approved
- [ ] Partner contacts and escalation paths documented

### Sales and customer success
- [ ] Sales pitch, demo and demo environment ready
- [ ] Pricing and packaging approved
- [ ] FAQ and objection handling written
- [ ] Customer onboarding / implementation playbook ready

### Support
- [ ] Support team trained
- [ ] Knowledge base articles published
- [ ] Escalation path to product and engineering defined

### Marketing and communications
- [ ] Website page, one-pager and product sheet ready
- [ ] Announcement copy drafted and **reviewed by Legal/Comms**
- [ ] Event or webinar plan (booth, talk, demo, customer story)
- [ ] Internal announcement planned
