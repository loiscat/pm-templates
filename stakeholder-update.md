# Stakeholder Update Template

A short, scannable update for leadership and cross-functional partners. Aim for something readable in **two minutes**. Lead with the answer, not the history.

Send weekly for fast-moving work, monthly for steady work. Keep the same format every time so readers know where to look.

> Drafts of company-wide or external communications should be reviewed by Legal/Comms before sending.

---

## Writing tips

- **Lead with status and the ask.** Executives often read only the first three lines.
- **Outcomes over activities.** "Pilot customer live" beats "worked on onboarding".
- **Be honest about amber and red.** An early warning with a plan builds trust; a late surprise destroys it.
- **One ask per update when possible.** If everything is urgent, nothing is.
- **Name owners and dates.** "Soon" and "the team" are not commitments.
- **Keep it short.** If a topic needs more, link to a document.

## Example (fictional)

**Subject:** Acme Support Hub — CRM integration update — 12 Mar — 🟡 At risk

**TL;DR:** The CRM integration is one week behind because the partner sandbox arrived late. We can still hit the launch date if we agree to ship without bulk import. **Decision needed from Product Leadership by Friday.**

**Progress:** Technical design approved. Authentication flow working in sandbox. Two pilot customers confirmed.

**Next:** Case sync build complete (22 Mar, Engineering). Pilot onboarding starts (25 Mar, Customer Success).

**Risk:** Partner API rate limits may affect large accounts. Mitigation: batching, load test planned for next week.

**Decision needed:** Ship v1 without bulk import (recommended) or move the launch by two weeks? Needed by Friday.
